01 About

I spend way too much time wondering “what if?”

I’m Aditya — UI/UX Designer who likes turning what ifs into things people can actually use. I’m curious about why people click, hesitate, get confused, or come back to something they love and I turn those little observations into cleaner flows, sharper interfaces, and experiences that feel almost obvious.

From a scribble on a blank canvas to a polished product that actually works.

I use AI to move faster, break away from obvious ideas, and experiment more. Because sometimes, the best design starts with a slightly stupid question.
